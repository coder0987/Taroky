Skyz, Mond, and Pagat

Basics of Taroky - the Terms

In Taroky, there are 22 "Tarocks" or trump cards. Each trump has a roman numeral on it, ranging from I-XXI. The Skyz (pronounced "skeesh"; sometimes called the "Fool"), unlike the other tarocks, doesn't have the numeral XXII - instead, its distinctive checkered pattern identifies it.

The Skyz

Aside from the Skyz, a few of the other Tarocks also have names. The XXI - the largest trump with a numeral - is also called the "Mond" (Pronounced moont), either because it featured the "Moon" or as a translation of "World".

The Mond

The lowest 4 - the I, II, III, and IIII - are called the "Birds" because they were often depcited with birds on the various cards. The lowest trump (the "I") is often called "Pagat" (pronounced pah-got or pog-ought), and awards bonus points to any team that can catch it on the last trick of the game.

The Pagat

Together, these three trumps - the Skyz, Mond, and Pagat - are known as the "Trul" (pronounced throol or trewl). While all of the other tarocks are each worth 1 point, the Trul are each worth 5. If you start a game with all three, you can call "Trul!" and receive 2 bonus chips from each other player!
