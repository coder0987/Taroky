106 Total points in the deck

  1. Calculate the points of all cards in your team's tricks
  2. Subtract your points from 53
  3. Add 10
  4. Double it (triple for prever)
  5. Divide by 10
  6. Round to the nearest whole number
  7. Pay out any special points
  8. Losers pay winners chips
