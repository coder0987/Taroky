Mission | MachTarok

Mission

MachTarok has a three part mission rooted in cultural education and intergenerational connection.

Cultural Education

Teach Taroky and its associated cultural traditions, such as art and language, using a variety of educational resources and methods.

Why we do this: To introduce new players to the game, give people who learned a long time ago or partially learned a way to get back into it, and teach seasoned players about other game variations.

How we do this: Full tutorials, articles, daily challenges, bots you can play against, and virtual rooms where you can practice even if you don't have 4 players physically present.

Preservation of the Game

Record variations of Taroky and where they are from (regions/tournaments), including different rules, decks, and forms of play.

Why we do this: To encourage participation in tournaments and regional play, keep less popular forms alive, and provide a platform for as many forms of Taroky as possible.

How we do this: Research into game variations in card game forums and at tournaments; articles based on user feedback and testimonials; customizable room and game settings.

Intergenerational Connection

Connect Taroky players from different backgrounds, ages, places, and environments who would not be able to play together in a traditional in person game.

Why we do this: This app was inspired by our grandmother, Helen Mach. During the COVID-19 pandemic, we played Scrabble together but missed playing Taroky. Family members who lived alone or far away also couldn't play and forgot the rules over time.

How we do this: With online play, anyone can play at any time of day or night from a phone, tablet, or desktop. Friends and family can join a specific room to play together. Bots of varying skill levels are available to fill odd seats.
